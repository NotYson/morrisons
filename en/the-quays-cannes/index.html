05 — The place

Welcome to The Quay's

On the Old Port since 2000, The Quay’s has seen many a sailor and deckhand come through. Its terrace facing the boats is the perfect spot for a drink in the late-afternoon sun, a stone’s throw from the beaches.

Inside, there’s football, rugby or hurling on screen, and the live music makes The Quay’s a must when you’re passing through Cannes.
