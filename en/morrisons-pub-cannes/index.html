05 — The place

Welcome to Morrison's Pub

Opened in 1997, Morrison’s is the first pub in Cannes: locals, expats and visitors meet here over a Guinness, a stone’s throw from Rue d’Antibes.

Yellow walls, worn wood and a terrace. Live Irish music and rock classics on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, and the big Premier League and rugby matches on screen. Happy hour every day from 5 to 8pm.
