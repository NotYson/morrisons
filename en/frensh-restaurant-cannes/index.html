05 — The place

Welcome to FREnSH

On Rue Félix Faure, right in the centre of Cannes, FREnSH serves fresh, simple French food from morning to night: coffee and juices to start the day, brasserie dishes at lunch and dinner.

On the terrace or inside, people come for the steak tartare, the croque-madame, the FREnSH burger or the rum baba, and for happy hour with a little sharing board.
